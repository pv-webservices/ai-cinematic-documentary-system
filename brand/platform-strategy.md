# Platform Strategy

## YouTube Shorts
Optimize the opening frame, spoken hook, subtitle readability and complete payoff within 20–60 seconds. Titles should promise the actual subject/reveal.

## Instagram Reels
Use the same editorial core with platform-native captions and minimal useful hashtags. Avoid engagement bait that weakens credibility.

## Long-form later
Expand successful research assets with additional sources, context, chronology and competing interpretations. Do not stretch a Short with repetition.

Publishing is manual in Phase 1.
