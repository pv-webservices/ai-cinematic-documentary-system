# Visual Direction

Cinematic but restrained. Visuals exist to explain, orient and create scale.

Prioritize composition, readable mobile framing, realistic materials, subject-appropriate atmosphere, consistent period/science constraints and intentional motion.

Use photorealistic reconstruction only when evidence supports it. Prefer maps, timelines, diagrams, cross-sections, labels, process animations or simplified simulations when they communicate more accurately.

Avoid random lens flare, constant camera motion, shallow depth-of-field everywhere, orange/teal grading by default, glossy AI skin, fake film grain, generic ruins/space imagery and dramatic inaccuracies.
