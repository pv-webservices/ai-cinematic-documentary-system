# Channel Strategy

## Positioning
Cinematic micro-documentaries built from verified research, initially focused on Science + History + Mysteries.

## Promise
Answer one compelling question per video with evidence, narrative progression and visual clarity.

## Phase 1
Prioritize repeatable editorial quality over volume. Publish manually after human QC. Build a durable research library so successful Shorts can become sequels or long-form documentaries.

## Avoid
Random facts, generic listicles, manufactured mysteries, fake urgency, unsupported certainty, copied documentary structure and visually impressive but misleading reconstruction.
