# Storytelling Principles

1. Open with a truthful question, consequence, image or counterintuitive fact.
2. Establish only the context needed to understand the next beat.
3. Escalate information: each beat should change or deepen the viewer's model.
4. Place caveats where they affect interpretation, not in a meaningless disclaimer dump.
5. Fulfill the hook.
6. End with a reveal, implication, unresolved boundary or changed perspective.
7. Cut any sentence that adds neither understanding nor tension.
8. Prefer concrete nouns, verbs, scales and comparisons over adjective-heavy prose.
