# Audience Profile

Primary viewers are curiosity-driven mobile audiences who enjoy science, history, engineering, archaeology and mysteries but do not require specialist knowledge. Assume intelligence, not expertise.

## Needs
- immediate reason to care;
- clear language;
- visual explanation;
- trustworthy framing;
- a satisfying implication or reveal.

Do not oversimplify into falsehood or rely on jargon to signal authority.
