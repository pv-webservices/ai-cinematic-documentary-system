# Visual Research — VID-0001
## Known constraints
- Artifact is made from surviving corroded bronze fragments; the complete original object is not preserved. [CLM-006]
- Gearing, inscriptions and back dials have been reconstructed from imaging/research, but not every original front component survives. [CLM-004, CLM-007]
- Rear eclipse display should emphasize the Saros spiral without inventing exact readable inscription text. [CLM-003]

## Safe visual strategy
Use stylized but physically plausible bronze reconstructions, clean diagrams and scan-inspired graphics. Avoid framing generated complete-device images as museum photography or archival evidence.

## Unknown / prohibit
- exact original wood-case appearance;
- precise missing front gearing without choosing a cited reconstruction;
- invented readable ancient Greek instructions;
- decorative gears that cannot mesh.
