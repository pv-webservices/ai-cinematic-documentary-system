# VID-0002 Hook Options

**Skill:** hook-writer-v1.0  
**Human gate:** SELECT ONE before scriptwriting.

## H01 — Visual mystery
- **Spoken hook:** “This Antarctic glacier looks like it’s bleeding. But the liquid coming out of it isn’t red at first.”
- **On-screen text:** WHY DOES THIS GLACIER “BLEED”?
- **Supporting claims:** CLM-002, CLM-003
- **Viewer question:** What is the liquid, and why does it turn red?
- **Fulfillment requirement:** explain iron-rich brine + oxidation/surface transformation.
- **Misleading risk:** LOW
- **Assessment:** strongest visual-first retention option.

## H02 — Unresolved mystery
- **Spoken hook:** “Scientists know why Blood Falls turns red. They still don’t fully know what makes it erupt.”
- **On-screen text:** ONE MYSTERY IS STILL UNSOLVED
- **Supporting claims:** CLM-003, CLM-008
- **Viewer question:** What has been solved, and what remains unknown?
- **Fulfillment requirement:** clearly separate color/pathway knowledge from release-trigger uncertainty.
- **Misleading risk:** LOW
- **Assessment:** strongest documentary/mystery positioning.

## H03 — Counterintuitive physics
- **Spoken hook:** “Scientists sampled liquid inside this Antarctic glacier at about minus seven degrees Celsius.”
- **On-screen text:** LIQUID AT −7°C
- **Supporting claims:** CLM-004, CLM-005
- **Viewer question:** How can it remain liquid?
- **Fulfillment requirement:** freezing-point depression + latent heat/cryoconcentration in simple language.
- **Misleading risk:** LOW
- **Assessment:** strongest pure-science hook, but the iconic red visual becomes secondary.

## H04 — Chemical reveal
- **Spoken hook:** “Blood Falls isn’t a red waterfall. It’s iron-rich brine changing color as it reaches the surface.”
- **On-screen text:** IT CHANGES COLOR HERE
- **Supporting claims:** CLM-002, CLM-003
- **Viewer question:** What chemical change creates the stain?
- **Fulfillment requirement:** modern mineralogy, no “just rust” simplification.
- **Misleading risk:** LOW
- **Assessment:** precise but less emotionally arresting than H01/H02.

## H05 — Hidden system
- **Spoken hook:** “Behind this red stain is a hidden network of salty water moving through frozen Antarctic ice.”
- **On-screen text:** WATER INSIDE THE GLACIER
- **Supporting claims:** CLM-004, CLM-005
- **Viewer question:** Where is it coming from, and why doesn’t it freeze solid?
- **Fulfillment requirement:** subglacial source + englacial pathways + cold-brine mechanism.
- **Misleading risk:** LOW
- **Assessment:** best for diagram-driven visual storytelling.

## H06 — Winter paradox
- **Spoken hook:** “Blood Falls can release brine even during the Antarctic winter, when surface melting can’t explain it.”
- **On-screen text:** IT FLOWS IN WINTER
- **Supporting claims:** CLM-007, CLM-008
- **Viewer question:** What drives the release?
- **Fulfillment requirement:** explain internal pressurized system and preserve unresolved trigger.
- **Misleading risk:** MEDIUM because “flow” can be misread as continuous.
- **Assessment:** strong but requires careful wording throughout.

## H07 — Historical correction
- **Spoken hook:** “When explorers saw this red glacier in 1911, algae seemed like an explanation. The real system is far stranger.”
- **On-screen text:** THE REAL BLOOD FALLS
- **Supporting claims:** CLM-009, CLM-002, CLM-004, CLM-006
- **Viewer question:** What actually causes it?
- **Fulfillment requirement:** explain brine, iron chemistry and hidden ecosystem.
- **Misleading risk:** LOW
- **Assessment:** richer history/science blend, but slower than H01 for Shorts.

## Hook recommendation
**Primary recommendation: H01.** It exploits the strongest authentic visual, introduces a precise contradiction immediately, and is fully answerable within 45 seconds.

**Second choice: H02.** Better if the channel should establish its identity as a documentary/mystery brand rather than maximize purely visual immediacy.
