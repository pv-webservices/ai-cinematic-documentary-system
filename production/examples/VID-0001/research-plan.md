# Research Plan — TOPIC-0001
## Central question
How did the Antikythera Mechanism encode astronomical cycles in gears to forecast eclipse possibilities?

## Sub-questions
1. When/where was it found and approximately when was it constructed?
2. Which astronomical functions are strongly established from surviving fragments?
3. What is the Saros cycle and how is it represented?
4. What modern imaging revealed the mechanism?
5. Which features are reconstructed or actively debated?

## High-risk claims
- exact construction date/origin;
- “first computer” superlative;
- exact front display/planetary gearing;
- saying eclipses were predicted exactly rather than as cycle-based possibilities.

## Primary/authoritative targets
Peer-reviewed Antikythera Mechanism research; National Archaeological Museum; academic review; UCL reconstruction publication summary.

## Stop condition
Proceed when eclipse/calendar mechanism, broad dating, discovery context and reconstruction limits are directly supported, with no major unresolved claim required by the Short.
