# Source Evaluation — TOPIC-0001

## SRC-001 — HIGH
Peer-reviewed Nature research using surface imaging and high-resolution X-ray tomography of surviving fragments. Direct support for known astronomical/calendar/eclipsing functions and complexity. Limitation: later research refined some reconstructions.

## SRC-002 — HIGH
Peer-reviewed Nature paper focused directly on back dials, Metonic calendar, Olympiad display and eclipse prediction. Strongest source here for the 223-lunar-month Saros dial claim.

## SRC-003 — HIGH
Peer-reviewed scholarly review synthesizing current knowledge as of 2018. Useful for distinguishing general agreement on solar/lunar functions from ongoing reconstruction work.

## SRC-004 — HIGH
National Archaeological Museum educational resource describing mechanism functions and astronomical cycles. Authoritative institutional context; use peer-reviewed papers for contested technical details.

## SRC-005 — MEDIUM
UCL institutional summary of a Scientific Reports reconstruction. Useful for current reconstruction framing, but the underlying paper should be preferred for detailed technical claims. Not required to support the core eclipse claim.
