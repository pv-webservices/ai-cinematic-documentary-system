## Score notes

### C01
Strong curiosity/emotion and reliable scientific source path. Risk is not low because the exact 1986 trigger remains unresolved and casualty language must be precise.

### C02
Excellent first engineering production: clear mechanism, strong visuals, strong paper trail. Emotional intensity is lower, but factual/production risk is comparatively manageable.

### C03
Lowest-risk candidate. NASA measurements make the central claim highly secure. Story depth is thinner than C04/C06/C12, so long-form expansion is less compelling.

### C04
Excellent mystery-to-mechanism structure. Risk comes from estimates of object size/composition/airburst altitude and historical evidence gaps.

### C05
Exceptional first-frame visual and low controversy. Must not overstate the still-uncertain plumbing/release mechanism below the glacier.

### C06
Best pure documentary story in the batch and strongest long-form bridge. Higher editorial burden: real deaths, Indigenous knowledge, historical interpretation, and unresolved final events.

### C07
Strong archaeology/geography identity and high originality potential. Reconstructions must clearly be labeled as reconstructions because shoreline/population details vary by period and model.

### C08
Visually excellent and easy to explain, but the narrative/emotional engine is weaker and exact emplacement history contains interpretation.

### C09
Very strong mechanism story with authoritative NPS sourcing and excellent explainable visuals. The main editorial risk is accidentally implying hydrothermal explosions mean a volcanic eruption is imminent.

### C10
Strong misconception-correction topic with authoritative sources. Risk comes from speculative claims about what a future reversal would do to technology, climate, or life.

### C11
Compelling, but human-remains sensitivity plus competing cause-of-death models make it a heavier first production.

### C12
Highest combined opportunity: scale, narrative, diagrams, and long-form runway. Risk is moderate because atmospheric kill mechanisms and their relative importance/duration require careful qualification.

## Recommended first-production shortlist
1. **C05 — Blood Falls**: best balance of curiosity, visual identity, accuracy, and low editorial risk.
2. **C02 — Roman concrete**: strongest engineering/ancient-technology fit and easiest clean Claim Ledger.
3. **C04 — Tunguska**: strongest mystery narrative, with manageable scientific uncertainty if phrased carefully.
4. **C09 — Yellowstone hydrothermal explosions**: excellent educational mechanism, but avoid current-hazard sensationalism.
5. **C12 — Chicxulub darkness**: largest cinematic ceiling, but a more complex evidence package than C05/C02.
