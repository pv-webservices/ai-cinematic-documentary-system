# VID-0001 Asset Provenance

## Visual classes
1. **DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE** — actual surviving artifact imagery, used only when licensing permits.
2. **RECONSTRUCTION** — AI/procedural complete-device visuals based on documented evidence.
3. **SCIENTIFIC VISUALIZATION** — gear, Saros, eclipse, scan and ratio diagrams created for explanation.
4. **HYPOTHETICAL** — not required for this production.

## Generated/reconstructed asset rules
- Any complete mechanism image must carry an internal production label `RECONSTRUCTION`; viewer-facing label should appear when ambiguity is possible.
- Do not invent readable Greek inscriptions.
- Do not imply scan-inspired graphics are genuine CT images.
- Do not use decorative gears that visibly interpenetrate or fail to mesh.
- Rear dial may simplify labels but must preserve the four-turn Saros spiral concept and 223-lunar-month explanation.
