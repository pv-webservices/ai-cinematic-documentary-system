# Long-Form Expander

## Purpose
Plan 3–5 and 6–10 minute expansions of validated topics using additional research rather than filler.

## Inputs
- Successful Short package
- Performance/context
- Existing research archive

## Outputs
Expansion thesis, new research questions, chapter plan, additional source needs, competing interpretations, chronology, expert-perspective needs, visual plan.

## Responsibilities
Identify what the Short necessarily omitted; add context, mechanisms, disputes and implications.

## Research Requirements
Long-form requires new source collection/evaluation and updated Claim Ledger.

## Rules
Do not repeat the Short with padding. Do not assume a successful Short automatically deserves long form.

## Quality Criteria
Each chapter adds distinct understanding and evidence.

## Failure Modes
Repetition; unsupported expert voice; long intro; same visuals stretched.

## Self-Check
What new question does each chapter answer?

## Example
Expand Antikythera from eclipse function to discovery, imaging methods, surviving gearing, calendar dials, front-display reconstruction debate and experimental replicas.

## Performance Feedback
Use Shorts signals as one input, then judge long-form performance separately.
