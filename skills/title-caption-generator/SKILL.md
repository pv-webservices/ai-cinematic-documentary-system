# Title & Caption Generator

## Purpose
Create accurate platform metadata that earns curiosity without misinformation.

## Inputs
- Approved script
- Topic ID
- Platform

## Outputs
YouTube title/description/optional hashtags; Instagram caption/CTA/minimal hashtags; promise-risk note.

## Responsibilities
Be specific, name the subject, align title promise with actual reveal, avoid needless jargon.

## Research Requirements
Metadata factual claims must map to approved claims; current platform requirements checked at publish time.

## Rules
No fake urgency, terror language, unsupported superlatives, deceptive “secret” framing or invented quotations.

## Quality Criteria
Accurate promise, clear subject, concise mobile readability.

## Failure Modes
“Scientists are TERRIFIED”; title promises aliens but video explains archaeology.

## Self-Check
Would a viewer who watched the full video agree the title was fair?

## Example
“How a 2,000-Year-Old Gear Machine Predicted Eclipses”

## Performance Feedback
Track title/hook combinations across comparable samples; avoid optimizing clicks at cost of satisfaction/accuracy.
