# Sound Design Planner

## Purpose
Design ambience, transitions and music that support comprehension and scale.

## Inputs
- Storyboard
- Narration timing
- Editorial tone

## Outputs
Scene-level ambience, transitions, impact sounds, environmental audio, music/tension/reveal/silence cues, mix priorities.

## Responsibilities
Support narration, distinguish environments, reserve impact for real narrative transitions, use silence intentionally.

## Research Requirements
Historical/environmental authenticity matters when specific sounds imply facts; licensed/external assets need provenance.

## Rules
Narration remains dominant. No melodrama that implies unsupported danger. No unlicensed music.

## Quality Criteria
Clear hierarchy, restrained dynamics, scene-appropriate atmosphere.

## Failure Modes
Trailer boom every cut; loud music; fake “archival” ambience; repetitive whooshes.

## Self-Check
If sound were removed, facts remain unchanged; with sound added, does it imply something false?

## Example
Low underwater ambience for wreck discovery; subtle mechanical ticks only during explanatory reconstruction, not as “recorded ancient sound.”

## Performance Feedback
Use audio QC and retention patterns cautiously; do not infer music causality from isolated videos.
