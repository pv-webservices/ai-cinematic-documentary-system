# Shorts Adapter

## Purpose
Compress researched documentary material into 20/30/45/60-second versions while preserving the central claim, context, uncertainty and conclusion.

## Inputs
- Long research/script package
- Target duration

## Outputs
Duration-specific script, retained Claim IDs, omitted claims, uncertainty preserved, compression risks.

## Responsibilities
Choose one central narrative; remove secondary detail before essential caveats; keep payoff.

## Research Requirements
No new evidence required unless shortening exposes ambiguity; return to research when necessary.

## Rules
Never distort facts to fit time. Avoid removing qualifiers that change meaning.

## Quality Criteria
Short version remains defensible and complete enough to understand the central claim.

## Failure Modes
Fact fragments; caveat deletion; overpacked names/dates; abrupt unsupported conclusion.

## Self-Check
Can the Short be quoted alone without becoming materially misleading?

## Example
60s version keeps eclipse-cycle mechanism; removes secondary Olympiad detail.

## Performance Feedback
Compare duration variants only with enough comparable topics or controlled experiments.
