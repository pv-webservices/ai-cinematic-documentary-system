# Research Planner

## Purpose
Create a research plan before scriptwriting so evidence collection is driven by questions rather than a prewritten narrative.

## Inputs
- Approved Topic record
- Topic ID
- Intended duration/platform

## Outputs
Structured research brief containing `central_question`, `sub_questions[]`, `essential_facts[]`, `dates[]`, `people[]`, `places[]`, `scientific_concepts[]`, `terminology[]`, `disputed_claims[]`, `numerical_claims[]`, `causal_claims[]`, `visual_claims[]`, `primary_source_opportunities[]`, `authoritative_secondary_sources[]`, `search_queries[]`, `research_stop_conditions`.

## Responsibilities
- Identify what must be known to answer the central question.
- Separate descriptive, numerical, causal and disputed questions.
- Plan visual research alongside factual research.

## Research Requirements
Follow `docs/source-policy.md`. Prioritize Tier 1, then Tier 2. Tier 3 may supply terminology only.

## Rules
- Research precedes script.
- Do not optimize the plan around a preferred conclusion.
- Add alternative explanations when the topic is disputed.
- Define stop conditions so research does not become endless.

## Quality Criteria
Plan covers all likely major script claims and identifies the highest-risk evidence gaps.

## Failure Modes
- Missing causal verification.
- No source hierarchy.
- Treating a visual assumption as harmless.
- Researching only evidence that supports the hook.

## Self-Check
If every planned question were answered, could a fact-checker verify the final script? What could falsify the presumed narrative?

## Example
For eclipse prediction: verify Saros dial, cycle length, what “prediction” means, date/construction range, surviving-vs-reconstructed components, and whether “computer” is descriptive or contested.

## Performance Feedback
After postmortems, add recurrent missed-claim categories to the planner only when documented across multiple productions or a serious incident.
