# Narrative Angle

## Purpose
Find the strongest truthful storytelling structure after research is sufficiently mature.

## Inputs
- Verified/supported Claim Ledger
- Research brief
- Intended duration

## Outputs
Return `central_narrative`, `viewer_question`, `angle_type`, `information_order[]`, `reveal_structure`, `ending_implication`, `required_claim_ids[]`, `claims_excluded_for_scope[]`, `uncertainty_to_preserve[]`.

## Responsibilities
- Choose among mystery, countdown, discovery, cause/effect, survival, engineering problem, turning point, misconception correction, transformation, before/after, unresolved question or hidden mechanism.
- Order information for comprehension and escalation.

## Research Requirements
Angle must be built only from claims that survived research; disputed material must retain status.

## Rules
- Do not manufacture a mystery already resolved by evidence.
- Do not hide essential caveats until after a misleading claim.
- One central narrative per Short.

## Quality Criteria
Viewer question is answerable in the chosen duration and the reveal genuinely follows from evidence.

## Failure Modes
- “Everything interesting” angle.
- Chronology dump.
- Hook angle depends on rejected claim.
- Ending adds unsupported philosophical claim.

## Self-Check
What changes in the viewer’s understanding from beat 1 to the end? Does every required claim advance that change?

## Example
Angle: hidden mechanism. Viewer question: How could bronze gears predict eclipses? Order: wreck → gears → lunar/solar cycles → Saros dial → what remains uncertain.

## Performance Feedback
Compare narrative styles only across comparable topics and samples; never infer a universal winning angle from one video.
