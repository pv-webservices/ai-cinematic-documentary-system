# Research Workflow
1. Create research plan.
2. Collect Tier 1 first, then Tier 2; Tier 3 only for discovery.
3. Assign SRC IDs.
4. Evaluate each source.
5. Extract atomic claims with CLM IDs.
6. Seek independent support for major causal/disputed/numerical claims where practical.
7. Record contradictions and newer evidence.
8. Mark claim statuses.
9. Stop when major script questions are answerable and high-risk contradictions are resolved or explicitly framed.

Gate: scriptwriting may begin only when the major Claim Ledger contains no silently unresolved premise.
