# Contributing

## Before changing production policy
1. Identify the canonical document or Skill.
2. Cite the evidence for the change: editorial incident, experiment, analytics sample, or authoritative external guidance.
3. Update the version notes when Skill behavior changes.
4. Avoid duplicating policy in multiple files; link to the canonical source.

## Pull-request checklist
- No secrets or credentials.
- Naming follows `docs/naming-conventions.md`.
- New factual examples use source and Claim IDs.
- No `UNCERTAIN`/`REJECTED` major claim is stated as fact.
- AI reconstructions are labeled internally.
- Analytics examples are clearly marked synthetic or left blank; production analytics are never fabricated.
- Automation changes preserve human approval gates.

## Documentation style
Concise, imperative, structured, evidence-oriented. Prefer schemas and checklists over vague advice.
