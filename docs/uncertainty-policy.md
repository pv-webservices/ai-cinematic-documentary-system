# Uncertainty Policy

Every important conclusion should be framed at the level justified by evidence.

- **FACT** — directly established/observed and not materially disputed for this use.
- **STRONG EVIDENCE** — multiple strong sources or robust evidence support the interpretation.
- **LIKELY INTERPRETATION** — evidence favors it, but alternatives remain credible.
- **ACTIVE DEBATE** — qualified experts/evidence materially disagree.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — testable proposal with limited/incomplete support.
- **SPECULATION** — plausible idea without adequate evidentiary support.
- **UNKNOWN** — evidence is insufficient to choose.

## Language discipline
Avoid upgrading evidence through wording. Replace “proved” with “found evidence,” “shows” with “suggests” where appropriate, and “caused” with “may have contributed” when causation is not established.

Simplification for duration is allowed only if it preserves the interpretation a reasonable expert would recognize.
