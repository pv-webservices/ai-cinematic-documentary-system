# Real People & Current Events Policy

Phase 1 defaults to evergreen science/history rather than breaking news.

If living people/current events are covered later:
- use current, directly relevant sources;
- do not fabricate quotes/actions/scandals;
- avoid unsupported accusations;
- distinguish allegation, evidence, official finding and opinion;
- never present synthetic scenes as genuine footage;
- require human factual/legal-risk review before publication.
