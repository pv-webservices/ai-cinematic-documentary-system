# Visual Accuracy Policy

Every scene should have an internal evidence label.

## RECONSTRUCTION
A visually plausible interpretation constrained by archaeological/historical/scientific evidence. It is not archival evidence.

## SCIENTIFIC VISUALIZATION
A simplified representation of a scientific process, scale or relationship. Simplifications must not invert or materially distort the mechanism being explained.

## HYPOTHETICAL VISUALIZATION
A clearly framed “what if” or counterfactual illustration. Do not intercut it as though it actually occurred.

## DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE
Actual archival image, footage, map, document, instrument recording or verified visual with provenance/licensing recorded.

## Rules
- Do not fabricate inscriptions, uniforms, tools, geography or device components merely for drama.
- Flag scale, chronology, geography and material constraints in visual research.
- If reconstruction confidence is low, use diagrams, labels, silhouettes, maps or close-up abstracted explanation rather than false specificity.
- Viewer-facing disclosure is required whenever omission would reasonably cause a synthetic reconstruction to be mistaken for genuine evidence.
